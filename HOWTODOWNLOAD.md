<h2>Install in Chrome</h2>
 </div>

1. Open `chrome://extensions` in Chrome.
2. Turn on **Developer mode** In the top right of your screen.
<img width="159" height="46" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e88315bc-f365-49a1-9c56-a45e5ceb73f6" />

4. Select **Load unpacked** In the top left of your screen.
<img width="196" height="113" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/79f05ded-bf83-4281-adc5-3c400eb82859" />

6. Choose the extracted Hydrogen Extension folder that contains `manifest.json`.
7. Confirm that Hydrogen appears in the extensions list and is enabled.

 <div align="center">
<h2>Use the extension</h2>
 </div>

1. Open or reload one of the supported sites:
   - [kirka.io](https://kirka.io)
   - [snipers.io](https://snipers.io)
   - [ask101math.com](https://ask101math.com)
   - [fpsiogame.com](https://fpsiogame.com)
   - [cloudconverts.com](https://cloudconverts.com)
3. Press the **Right Shift** key to open or close the Hydrogen menu. Press **Escape** to close it (if Right shift isn't working)
4. Use the menu tabs to adjust the available styles, plugins, and client settings. Changes are saved by the extension.

The extension does not open a popup from the browser toolbar; its menu is displayed on supported websites.

 <div align="center">
<h2>Update or Remove</h2>
 </div>

- **Update:** Download and extract the latest repository ZIP, then use **Load unpacked** to select the updated folder. If the browser already has the old folder loaded, load the new one and remove the old entry from the extensions page.
- **Remove:** Open the browser's extensions page, find Hydrogen, and select **Remove**.
